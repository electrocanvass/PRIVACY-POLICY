Auto Ad Skipper – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 NOVEMBER 2026

Auto Ad Skipper does not collect, store on any server, send, or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

What the app does

Auto Ad Skipper taps the “Skip Ad” button in the YouTube app when it appears, and mutes media volume while a video ad plays. It then restores your volume when your video resumes.

Accessibility service

The app uses Android’s AccessibilityService API for two purposes only:

  • Detecting the “Skip Ad” button in the YouTube app and tapping it for you.
  • Detecting when a video ad is playing, so audio can be muted and then restored.

Screen content is processed on your device in real time. It is never saved, logged, or sent anywhere. The app does not read your messages, passwords, photos, or search history, and it does not track what you watch.

You turn this service on yourself in Android Settings, and you can turn it off at any time: from the floating button, the Quick Settings tile, the app, or Android’s Accessibility settings.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your settings (for example, the position of the floating button and your “Pause on App Open” choice) and a count of ads skipped. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Accessibility service: to detect and tap “Skip Ad” and to detect ads, as described above.
  • Display over other apps: to show the floating pause/resume button.
  • Foreground service: to keep the floating button running reliably.
  • Notifications: to show the service status notification on Android 13 and later.

The app does not request the internet permission.

Ads, analytics, and third parties

The app contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking tools. No data is shared with third parties.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
